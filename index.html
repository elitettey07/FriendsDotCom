The Cohort is a community for university mates who are years into their careers and still chipping away at a degree — built so nobody has to do the hard parts alone.

Join the cohort Free to join. No dues, no meetings you can't skip.

Mission

We help people finish what they started — without asking them to put their careers, families, or sleep on hold to do it.

Most of us are five, ten, fifteen years out of undergrad — back in lecture halls after work, raising kids between assignments, studying on lunch breaks. The Cohort exists so that juggling act doesn't have to be a solo one.

Vision

We want a future where going back to school later in life isn't a lonely, invisible effort — where the people who know exactly what it's like to submit an assignment from a hospital waiting room, or study for finals on a lunch break, are the same people cheering you on.

Ten years from now, we picture a Cohort chapter in every city our old classmates have scattered to — each one still run by the people who show up, not by a committee.

Goals

  • Weekly study sessions

    Two evenings a week, in person or on a call, so nobody's cramming solo at midnight.

  • Peer mentorship

    Members further along in their degree paired with those just starting — no formal application needed.

  • A shared resource library

    Old notes, reading lists, and "here's what I wish I knew" advice, passed down instead of lost.

  • Career-and-classroom crossover

    Using what we've learned at work to make the coursework easier, and the coursework to sharpen what we do at work.

  • Marking the wins

    Every graduation, every passed exam, every promotion gets celebrated together — not scrolled past on social media.
